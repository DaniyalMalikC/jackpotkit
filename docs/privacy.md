# Privacy and telemetry

JackpotKit has zero default telemetry and no JackpotKit-controlled backend. Library code must not transmit analytics, results, identifiers, device information, or usage information.

Applications may connect their own analytics through explicit callbacks. Those integrations remain application-owned and must follow the application's privacy policy and consent requirements.
