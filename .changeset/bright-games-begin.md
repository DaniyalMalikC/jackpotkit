Add the Phase 1 platform-independent primitives and deterministic testing toolkit.

Core now includes generic results and rewards, lifecycle vocabulary, typed events, injectable and seeded random sources, sync or async result providers, typed errors, and composable validation helpers. Testing adds sequence-based randomness, a call-capturing mock result provider, and deterministic factories.
